What is Padel Kulture

Concept before
cloth

Padel Kulture is an independent workshop that treats the language of the sport — its shots, its rules, its glass — as material worth printing.

01 / Concept

One shot, one mark

Every design starts with a real move on the court — the víbora, the bandeja, the glass rebound — reduced to a single line worth wearing.

02 / Craft

Hand screen-printed

Every tee is pulled one squeegee pass at a time in our workshop, so the ink sits thick and holds its color long after the hundredth wash.

03 / Scarcity

Numbered, then retired

Each concept is printed once, in a run of sixty, numbered on the inside neck — then the screen is retired for good.
